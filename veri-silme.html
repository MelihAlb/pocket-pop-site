Nasıl talep edilir

Üç yolu var. İlk ikisi anında ve kendi elinizde, üçüncüsü için bize yazmanız gerekiyor.

  1. Oyunun içinden. Pocket Pop'u açın › başlık ekranının sağ üstündeki dişli simgesine dokunun › Hesabını sil › Sil. Bu, aşağıdaki tabloda "cihazda tutulanlar" diye geçen her şeyi anında siler.
  2. Uygulamayı kaldırarak. Pocket Pop'u telefonunuzdan kaldırın, ya da Ayarlar › Uygulamalar › Pocket Pop › Depolama › Verileri temizle deyin. Cihazdaki her şey gider.
  3. Bize yazarak. alb.studiolab@gmail.com adresine, konu satırına "Veri silme talebi" yazarak e-posta gönderin. Talebi 30 gün içinde yerine getirir ve size yazılı olarak dönüş yaparız. Oyun hesap açmanızı istemediği için sizi tanımlayan bir kaydımız yok; bu yolu yalnızca istatistik kayıtlarının silinmesini istiyorsanız kullanmanız gerekir.

English

This page is for Pocket Pop, a game made by ALB Studio. It explains how to request deletion of your data, what is deleted, and what is kept and for how long.

How to request deletion

There are three ways. The first two are immediate and entirely in your hands; the third needs an email to us.

  1. From inside the game. Open Pocket Pop › tap the gear icon at the top right of the title screen › Delete your account › Delete. This immediately deletes everything listed as "on your phone" in the table below.
  2. By uninstalling. Remove Pocket Pop from your phone, or go to Settings › Apps › Pocket Pop › Storage › Clear data. Everything on the device is gone.
  3. By writing to us. Email alb.studiolab@gmail.com with "Data deletion request" in the subject line. We will action it within 30 days and reply in writing. The game never asks you to create an account, so we hold no record that identifies you; use this route only if you want past analytics records deleted.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhereWhat happens
Best score, Adventure progress, lives, duel opponents and records, this device's duel identity On your phone only Deleted — immediately, by the in-game deletion or by uninstalling. None of it is ever sent to a server.
Language, sound, music, haptics and statistics preferences On your phone only Kept by the in-game deletion — these are choices you made about how the app behaves, not data about you. The statistics switch especially: turning collection back on for somebody who had turned it off would be the opposite of honouring a deletion request. Uninstalling removes these too.
Live duel room: anonymous sign-in id, board seed, turn number, move played, score, timestamps Firebase Realtime Database (Europe, europe-west1) Deleted as soon as the match ends. If your connection drops, the server deletes it for you. If nobody joins, the room expires after 2 minutes. Nothing is retained.
Anonymous usage statistics: app opening, run and level starts and results, errors; alongside the device model, OS version, country and a random app-specific identifier that Google attaches Google Analytics (Firebase) Kept for the retention period configured for this project (no more than 14 months), then deleted automatically. You can switch collection off in the game's Settings; while it is off, no events are sent. To have past records deleted, use route 3 above.
Advertising ID, IP address, general device information Google AdMob Never reaches us; processed by Google directly, so we hold no record to delete. You can reset or delete the identifier in Settings › Google › Ads. Google's retention policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

The game never collects your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and never asks you to create an account. Full details in the privacy policy.
